### 📌 Project Executive Summary
This project involved a deep-dive analysis of **9,900+ transactions** from a global supermarket chain. By transforming raw data into a structured analytical model, I identified critical operational gaps—most notably where high sales volume was actually resulting in net losses due to inefficient discounting.

### 🛠️ Technical Workflow & Tools
- **Microsoft Excel (Advanced):** Final interactive dashboard with integrated Pivot Tables and Slicers.
- **Power Query:** Performed ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) to clean messy records and standardize 22 unique data columns.
- **Financial Modeling:** Developed custom calculations for **Profit Margin %**, **Average Discount Impact**, and **Regional Contribution**.

### 📈 Strategic Business Insights
- **Revenue Engine:** Identified **Technology** as the primary profit driver (17.4% margin), recommending a 15% increase in inventory allocation for this category.
- **Profit At-Risk:** Discovered that **Furniture** maintains a razor-thin **2.4% margin**; proposed a revised shipping and discount strategy to recover lost revenue.
- **The Loss-Leader Trap:** Isolated **Texas and Pennsylvania** as high-risk zones, where aggressive discounting led to a combined **net loss of ~$40,000**.
- **Peak Seasonality:** December generates **5x more revenue** than February, identifying a critical window for seasonal marketing spend.
